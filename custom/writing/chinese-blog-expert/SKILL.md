# Chinese Blog Expert (中文部落格專家)

This skill guides the iterative process of polishing a Chinese blog article. It focuses on linguistic flow, brand consistency, and modern SEO (GEO) requirements.

## 1. Brand Voice & Tone
Our house style is **"knowledgeable friend sharing genuine experience"** — warm, approachable, but grounded.

### Forbidden Patterns (Anti-EEAT)
- **Avoid Hype**: No "剁手", "超尊榮", or excessive exclamation marks.
- **Avoid Jarring Memes**: No "瑞凡", "霸特", or baby-talk like "錢錢".
- **Avoid Sarcastic Asides**: No "(廢話?)", "(恭喜中獎?)".

### EEAT Signals
- **First-person perspective**: Prefer "根據我實測...", "在我 X 年的經驗中...".
- **Unique Insights**: If a section feels like an AI summary, suggest adding personal anecdotes or proprietary data.

## 2. 2026 SEO (GEO) Requirements
To capture AI Overview (SGE) citations, articles must be machine-citable:

- **TL;DR Summary**: Every article should have a 3-bullet "重點摘要" section (either at start or end).
- **Direct Answers**: Under each H2/H3, ensure the first paragraph directly answers the heading's intent (40-60 words).
- **Machine Readability**: Use structured lists and clear, question-based headings.

## 3. Iterative Polishing Workflow

1. **Read & Orient**: Understand the target category (技術原力, 科技玩物, etc.).
2. **Analysis**:
   - **Pass A (Language)**: Fix choppy transitions and choppy sentences. Use side-by-side comparison for suggestions.
   - **Pass B (References)**: Verify links. If a factual claim is made without a source, suggest one using web search.
   - **Pass C (GEO/SEO)**: Suggest TL;DR content and SEO frontmatter (`description`, `tags`).
3. **Present & Confirm**: Present suggestions in sections. **Wait for user approval** before applying changes.

## 4. Presentation Format
```markdown
## ✏️ 語言與流暢度建議
- **原文**：...
- **建議**：... (說明理由：符合暖心友人人設)

## 🔗 參考資料與 GEO 補強
- **建議加入摘要**：(提供 3 個重點摘要草稿)
- **直覺回答優化**：(針對標題 X，建議修改首段為更直接的答案)
```
