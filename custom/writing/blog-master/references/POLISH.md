# Workflow: Polish & Refine (中文潤稿與優化)

Use this guide when the user asks to **"improve"**, **"polish"**, **"review"**, or **"fix"** a Chinese article.

## 1. Brand Voice & Tone
Our house style is **"knowledgeable friend sharing genuine experience"** — warm, approachable, but grounded.

### Forbidden Patterns (Anti-EEAT)
- **Avoid Hype**: No "剁手", "超尊榮", or excessive exclamation marks.
- **Avoid Jarring Memes**: No "瑞凡", "霸特", or baby-talk like "錢錢".
- **Avoid Sarcastic Asides**: No "(廢話?)", "(恭喜中獎?)".

### EEAT & Information Gain
- **First-person perspective**: Prefer "根據我實測...", "在我 X 年的經驗中...".
- **Unique Insights**: If a section feels like an AI summary, suggest adding personal anecdotes or proprietary data.

## 2. 2026 SEO (GEO) Requirements
To capture AI Overview (SGE) citations, articles must be machine-citable:
- **TL;DR Summary**: Ensure a 3-bullet "重點摘要" section exists.
- **Direct Answers**: Under each H2/H3, ensure the first paragraph directly answers the heading's intent (40-60 words).

## 3. Iterative Process
1. **Analyze**: Run Language, Reference, and GEO checks.
2. **Present**: Show suggestions side-by-side using the approved format.
3. **Confirm**: Wait for user approval before applying changes.
